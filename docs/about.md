# Questions, About, and the Honest Disclaimers

A wrapped-up corner: your printable second-opinion script, the disclaimer stated plainly, and who made this.

## The printable second-opinion script

Print this page or the snippet below, and hand it to (or read to) a second shop:

> **Second-opinion request**
>
> Car: [year, make, model, mileage]
> My concern: [symptom, or "check engine light on — code P____"]
>
> I have a written estimate from another shop for the following work: [paste or list].
>
> **What I'm asking of you:** diagnose my car from scratch — don't price their list. Tell me what you find, what you'd recommend, and what it would cost. I'm happy to pay your diagnostic fee for your time today. I will not be offended if you find something different.
>
> Three questions I will definitely ask:
> 1. What test or measurement confirms the failing part — not just the check-engine code?
> 2. Which items on your list are safety-critical today, and which can wait?
> 3. Does your diagnostic fee credit toward the repair if I book the work with you?

Carrying this on paper dramatically improves how any honest shop treats the visit, because it signals *"this one reads things before signing."*

## The disclaimer — education, never diagnosis

**This guide is educational material, not mechanical, safety, financial, or legal advice.**

- We explain vocabulary, typical structures, typical processes, and typical decisions. We never tell you what is broken on **your specific car**, because only a qualified professional who inspects your actual vehicle can determine that.
- Prices and ranges described anywhere in this guide are illustrative and vary by region, vehicle, year, shop, and time. Treat them as context, never as quotes.
- Nothing in this guide overrides a professional's statement that something on your car is unsafe. If a professional tells you a brake, tire, steering, or fuel-system component is unsafe, that's a class of decision that doesn't get shopped or deferred.
- AI usage described in [AI Quote Translator](ai-translator.md) is a translation and coaching tool. It does not diagnose your car, and neither does this guide. Any apparently concrete claim from an AI about *your* car is speculation until verified hands-on.
- Your owner's manual, the sticker in your door jamb, and the government recall lookup (free, at nhtsa.gov) outrank anything written here, including by us — including this very page.

If any of this feels in tension with your actual situation, that's a *precise* sign to trust the professional standing in front of your raised hood, and to use this guide only to ask clearer questions.
